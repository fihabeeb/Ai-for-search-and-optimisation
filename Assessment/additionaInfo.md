1. You are required to implement both algorithms and compare their performance in addressing the
optimization challenge.
2. You will need to evaluate each algorithm's solution quality, computation time, and robustness to
determine how effectively it solves the problem.
3. You must critically evaluate the performance of both algorithms, highlight their strengths and
weaknesses, and discuss why one algorithm may outperform the other for the given problem.
4. You must demonstrate scalability by testing both algorithms on problem instances containing:
• 10 cities
• 20 cities
• 30 cities
• 40 cities
• 50 cities
For each problem size, evaluate and compare:
• Solution quality (route length)
• Runtime performance
• Convergence behaviour
• Robustness across repeated runs
• Computational scalability
Students should use tables and visualisations to clearly present and discuss performance trends as
problem size increases
